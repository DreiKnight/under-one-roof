# Product Brief — Under One Roof

## Working Name

**Under One Roof**

## Tagline Options

- Everything your home needs, under one roof.
- Your home finally has a memory.
- Bills, documents, repairs, and reminders — all under one roof.
- The smarter way to manage where you live.

## One-Sentence Description

Under One Roof is an AI-powered home management app that helps renters and homeowners organize bills, contracts, documents, maintenance, repairs, renewals, negotiation opportunities, and emergency information in one place.

## The Problem

People do not have one reliable place to manage the life of their home.

Renters lose track of:

- Lease terms
- Renewal dates
- Security deposit protections
- Utility setup and cancellation
- Renter’s insurance
- Maintenance requests
- Move-in/move-out documentation

Homeowners lose track of:

- Maintenance schedules
- Contractor history
- Warranties
- Insurance renewals
- Utility trends
- Repair costs
- Appliance manuals
- Emergency shutoff locations
- Property documents

Most people use a messy mix of email, screenshots, PDFs, paper folders, calendar reminders, and memory.

## The Product Vision

Under One Roof becomes the digital brain for a home.

A user should be able to ask:

- When does my lease renew?
- When is my internet contract up?
- Did my bill increase?
- When should I change my HVAC filter?
- Who fixed the sink last time?
- Is my appliance still under warranty?
- What does my lease say about pets?
- What should I do before winter?
- What should I do if my pipe bursts?

And the app should answer from the user’s saved information.

## Target Users

### Primary MVP Users

1. Renters
   - Apartment renters
   - Roommates
   - First-time renters
   - People moving soon

2. First-time homeowners
   - People overwhelmed by maintenance
   - People who recently bought a house
   - People who need structure and reminders

### Future Users

3. Small landlords
4. Property managers
5. Real estate agents
6. Home service providers
7. Insurance agents
8. HOAs

## Product Positioning

Do not position this as only a maintenance app.

Better positioning:

> Home life management.

> A personal operating system for where you live.

> One place for every bill, document, reminder, contract, repair, and home decision.

## Competitive Angle

Many products solve one narrow issue:

- Budgeting apps track money.
- Cloud storage holds documents.
- Calendar apps hold reminders.
- Contractor apps help find service providers.
- Property management apps serve landlords.

Under One Roof combines the user’s home information into one AI-assisted management system.

## The Emotional Hook

People are not buying reminders. They are buying peace of mind.

The app should feel calm, helpful, and protective.

## The Main Product Promise

Never forget, lose, overpay, or feel unprepared for anything related to your home again.
