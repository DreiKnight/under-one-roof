# User Stories — Under One Roof

## Renter Stories

### Lease Management

As a renter, I want to upload my lease so I can quickly find important terms later.

As a renter, I want to know when my lease ends so I do not miss renewal or move-out deadlines.

As a renter, I want reminders before my lease renewal window so I can decide whether to stay or move.

As a renter, I want to know what my lease says about pets, guests, parking, fees, and move-out rules.

### Security Deposit

As a renter, I want to take move-in photos so I can document the apartment’s condition.

As a renter, I want a move-out checklist so I can protect my security deposit.

As a renter, I want to store photos and notes of existing damage so I have proof later.

### Bills

As a renter, I want to track rent and utilities so I know what is due and when.

As a renter with roommates, I want to track shared bills so we can split costs clearly.

### Maintenance Requests

As a renter, I want to log maintenance issues so I remember what I reported and when.

As a renter, I want the app to help me write a message to my landlord or property manager.

## Homeowner Stories

### Maintenance

As a homeowner, I want the app to recommend maintenance tasks based on my home type and season.

As a homeowner, I want reminders for HVAC filters, gutters, smoke detectors, dryer vents, and seasonal tasks.

As a homeowner, I want to track what I completed and how much it cost.

### Repairs

As a homeowner, I want to log repairs so I remember who did the work, what it cost, and whether there is a warranty.

As a homeowner, I want to store before-and-after photos for repairs.

As a homeowner, I want to know when a repair warranty expires.

### Contractors

As a homeowner, I want to save trusted contractors so I can find them later.

As a homeowner, I want to rate contractors and attach invoices to their work.

### Documents

As a homeowner, I want to store inspection reports, insurance policies, warranties, manuals, receipts, and permits.

As a homeowner, I want to search my documents by asking natural questions.

## Shared User Stories

### Dashboard

As a user, I want a dashboard that shows only what matters today.

As a user, I want to see upcoming bills, renewals, and tasks in one place.

### Negotiation

As a user, I want to know when a bill or contract may be negotiable.

As a user, I want scripts for calling providers to lower bills.

As a user, I want to see when a promotional rate may be ending.

### Emergency

As a user, I want to store emergency home information like shutoff locations and important contacts.

As a user, I want the app to guide me during a home emergency without making me search everywhere.

## Future Stories

As a landlord, I want to manage multiple rental units.

As a property manager, I want to track tenant issues and maintenance requests.

As a contractor, I want to send work records directly to a homeowner’s home history.

As a real estate agent, I want to help buyers create a home profile during closing.
