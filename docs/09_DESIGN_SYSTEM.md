# Design System — Under One Roof

## Design Personality

The app should feel like:

- Calm
- Warm
- Trustworthy
- Clean
- Organized
- Helpful
- Slightly premium

This app manages sensitive home information. It should not feel playful in a childish way. It should feel reassuring.

## Visual Inspiration

- Apple: simplicity and spacing
- Notion: organization and flexible information
- Rocket Money: bills and financial clarity
- Airbnb: warmth and trust
- Linear: clean modern software feel

## Layout Principles

- Mobile-first
- Card-based dashboard
- Clear hierarchy
- Plenty of white space
- Few colors
- Strong empty states
- Friendly helper text

## Suggested Color Direction

Possible palette:

- Warm off-white background
- Deep charcoal text
- Soft beige cards
- Muted green accent
- Calm blue for informational states
- Amber for attention/reminders
- Red only for urgent/emergency items

## Typography

Use a clean sans-serif font.

Possible choices:

- Inter
- Geist
- System font stack

## UI Components Needed

- App shell
- Sidebar or bottom nav
- Top bar
- Card
- Button
- Badge
- Empty state
- Modal
- Form field
- Date picker
- File upload area
- Reminder card
- Insight card
- Status pill
- Dashboard metric card
- Timeline item

## Dashboard Cards

Cards should answer specific questions:

- What bill is due next?
- What contract renews soon?
- What maintenance is overdue?
- What document was added recently?
- What should I do next?

## Tone of Voice

Helpful but not robotic.

Good:

- “Your internet contract renews soon. This may be a good time to negotiate.”
- “Your renter’s insurance expires in 18 days.”
- “You have 3 home tasks due this month.”

Avoid:

- “URGENT ACTION REQUIRED” unless it is truly urgent.
- Legal or financial certainty.
- Fear-based language.

## Empty State Examples

Bills page:

> No bills added yet. Add your rent, mortgage, utilities, or insurance so Under One Roof can help you stay ahead.

Contracts page:

> Start with your lease, internet contract, insurance policy, or home warranty. We’ll help you track dates and renewal windows.

Documents page:

> Keep leases, receipts, manuals, warranties, and home records here so they’re easy to find later.

Maintenance page:

> Add your first maintenance task or let us suggest a starter checklist based on your home.
